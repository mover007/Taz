# Lotus Atoll

Settings are in the **Lotus Atoll** category of `/taz`. Everything shows only while you're on the Atoll.

## Trophy Frogs

A table of all 12 species, laid out like SkyHanni's Trophy Fish display: each frog's icon (from Hypixel's resource pack), its name in its rarity's colour, how many you've caught in total and of each tier (`B S G D`), and your catches toward the Gold pity (100, or 50 for the Exploding Frog and the Puddle Jumper) and then the Diamond pity (600, or 300). A tier you have that Taz has no count for shows ✔. The header shows your 48 and the tier Researcher Ribery rewards next. Under it: this session's frogs and the Lotus Ribery gives for them, and your Lily-splosions with their Lotus. **Pity column** hides the last column.

Which tiers you have comes from the **Trophy Frogs** widget in the tab list, so the HUD always matches your collection on any computer. How many you've caught, and so the pity counts, comes from Minecraft's chat logs, so frogs from before Taz count. Logs stay on the computer you played on, so a second computer counts only its own catches. **Re-read** counts them from scratch.

## Frog guide

A HUD of what to fish for next:

- **Can bite here:** the frogs you can catch where you're standing right now.
- **Rain and blessing:** whether it's raining (Wetlands Frogs) and how long your Frogcoin blessing has left (Blessed Frogs; 30 minutes from Hypixel's "WISE!" message).
- **Still to catch:** each frog you're missing at your tier, with how to get it. ✔ means you're set up for it now, ✘ means you aren't, and • means Taz can't tell.

| Frog | How |
|---|---|
| Common Frog | Anywhere |
| Exploding Frog | Push Lily Pads together until one explodes |
| Leap Frog | Be in the air when it bites |
| Wetlands Frog | Fish in the rain |
| Reality Hopper | Fish in a Wormhole (only seen with Froggles on) |
| Blessed Frog | Throw a Frogcoin into the Lotus Eater's Cave pond first |
| Bullfrog | Wear a Red Sweater |
| Sea Frog | Fish while you're underwater |
| Cave Frog | Fish in the Lotus Eater's Cave |
| Highlands Frog | Fish in the Lotus Highlands (Researcher Kay launches you up) |
| Tree Frog | Stand on leaves |
| Puddle Jumper | Cast into the Puddle Jumper sea creature and hold on |

## Waypoints

- **Places:** Researcher Ribery, Researcher Betty, the Lotus Eater's Cave, Researcher Kay (the launch up to the Highlands), the Lotus Highlands and the Tewtil Tunnel.
- **Wormhole spots:** the 21 spots a Wormhole can open at (two are open at a time), shown while you wear Froggles.
- **Fairy Souls:** the Atoll's 6, the same way as Galatea's (colour, pointer and hints are in Galatea > Fairy Souls).

## Alerts

Titles for every Gold and Diamond frog and each frog new to your collection, for a Frog Prince or a Puddle Jumper, and a minute before your Frogcoin blessing ends. **Tell your party** also says them in party chat.
