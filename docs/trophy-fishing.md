# Trophy Fishing

Settings are in the **Trophy Fishing** category of `/taz`. Everything shows only while you're on the Crimson Isle.

## Trophy Fish

A table of all 18 fish, laid out like SkyHanni's Trophy Fish display: each fish's icon (from Hypixel's resource pack), its name in its rarity's colour, how many you've caught in total and of each tier (`B S G D`), and your catches toward the Gold pity (100) and then the Diamond pity (600). A tier you have that Taz has no count for shows ✔. The header shows your 72 and the lowest tier you haven't finished. Under it: this session's fish and the Magmafish they fillet into.

Where the counts come from:

- **Odger's Trophy Fish menu.** Open it once and Taz reads every fish's counts from it, then adds each catch after that. Open it again any time to sync.
- **The tab list.** Its Trophy Fish widget says which tiers you have.
- **Your chat logs.** Until you open Odger's menu, Taz counts the catches in Minecraft's chat logs, which stay on the computer you played on. **Re-read** counts them from scratch.

**Only missing** hides fish you have at every tier, and **Pity column** hides the last column.

## Fish guide

A HUD of what to fish for next:

- **Can bite here:** the fish you can catch where you're standing right now.
- **Volcano, mana and bobber:** the Blazing Volcano's state from the tab list, your mana from the action bar, and how long your bobber has been out.
- **Still to catch:** each fish you're missing at your tier, with how to get it. ✔ means you're set up for it now, ✘ means you aren't, and • means Taz can't tell (bait, geysers, eruptions).

| Fish | How |
|---|---|
| Sulphur Skitter | Within 4 blocks of Sulphur (the sponge blocks) |
| Obfuscated-1 | Fish with Corrupted Bait |
| Steaming-Hot Flounder | Bobber by a geyser in the Blazing Volcano |
| Gusher | Blazing Volcano, 7 to 16 minutes after it erupts |
| Blobfish | Anywhere |
| Obfuscated-2 | Use Obfuscated-1 as bait |
| Slugfish | Leave the bobber in for 20 seconds or more |
| Flyfish | Fish from 8 or more blocks above the lava in the Blazing Volcano |
| Obfuscated-3 | Use Obfuscated-2 as bait |
| Lavahorse | Anywhere |
| Mana Ray | Have 1,200 mana or more |
| Volcanic Stonefish | Fish in the Blazing Volcano |
| Vanille | A Starter Lava Rod with no enchantments |
| Skeleton Fish | Fish in the Burning Desert or Dragontail |
| Moldfin | Fish in the Mystic Marsh |
| Soul Fish | Fish in the Stronghold |
| Karate Fish | Fish in the Dojo |
| Golden Fish | Shows up after 8 minutes of fishing; hook it until it's weak |

Catch conditions and fillet values are from the SkyHanni repo's trophy fish data.

## Alerts

Titles for every Gold and Diamond fish and each fish new to your collection, when a Golden Fish surfaces, and when it's weak enough to reel in. **Tell your party** also says Gold and Diamond fish in party chat.
