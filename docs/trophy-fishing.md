# Trophy Fishing

Settings are in the **Trophy Fishing** category of `/taz`. Everything shows only while you're on the Crimson Isle.

## Trophy Fish

A table of all 18 fish, laid out like SkyHanni's Trophy Fish display: each fish's icon (from Hypixel's resource pack), its name in red by rarity (light red for common, getting darker up to the Golden Fish), how many you've caught in total and of each tier (`B S G D`, the same reds from light Bronze to dark Diamond), and your catches toward the Gold pity (100) and then the Diamond pity (600). A tier you have that Taz has no count for shows ✔.

A higher tier counts for the ones below it: a Diamond Flyfish with no Gold does for Gold, and its missing tiers show a grey ✔. The header shows your 72 and the lowest tier not every fish has reached. Under it: this session's fish and the Magmafish they fillet into.

Where the counts come from:

- **Odger's Trophy Fish menu.** Open it once and Taz reads every fish's counts from it, then adds each catch after that. Open it again any time to sync.
- **The tab list.** Its Trophy Fish widget says which tiers you have.
- **Your chat logs.** Until you open Odger's menu, Taz counts the catches in Minecraft's chat logs, which stay on the computer you played on. It reads Hypixel's lines and SkyHanni's rewritten ones ("TROPHY FISH! 204th BRONZE Blobfish (276th total)"). **Re-read** counts them from scratch.

**Only missing** hides fish you have at every tier, and **Pity column** hides the last column.

## Fish guide

A HUD of what to fish for next:

- **Can bite here:** the fish you can catch where you're standing right now.
- **Volcano, mana and bobber:** the Blazing Volcano's state from the tab list, your mana from the action bar, and how long your bobber has been out.
- **Still to catch:** each fish you're missing at your tier, with how to get it. ✔ means you're set up for it now, ✘ means you aren't, and • means Taz can't tell (bait, geysers).

**Gushers:** Taz watches the volcano in the tab list. When an eruption ends (it goes back to INACTIVE), the guide counts down to the Gusher window ("opens in 3m"), then shows how long is left ("6m left"). If you joined the lobby after the eruption, Taz can't tell until you catch a Gusher: then it's Gusher time for up to 9 minutes.

## Alerts

Titles for every Gold and Diamond fish and each fish new to your collection, when a Golden Fish surfaces, and when it's weak enough to reel in. **Tell your party** also says Gold and Diamond fish in party chat.
