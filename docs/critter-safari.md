# Critter Safari

Turn on the **Critter Safari** category in `/taz` and Taz walks you through a run.

## Getting in

Near the Safari entrance in Torrhus Canyon, a HUD lists what you need (Hunting XV and a Safari Ticket from Miria's Starlyn Contests) and a beam points to the Safari Manager.

## Finding critters

- **Highlights:** critters you still need glow red with a name tag and their distance (`Foxtrot ✦ new 23m`, where "new" means you've never caught one). Sparkling ones glow gold and get a title. Pick which critters count: not caught this run (default), never caught by you, or all.
- **Last seen:** when a needed critter goes out of view, a faint marker stays where it was for 90 seconds, or until you get there.
- **Throw preview** (off by default): while you hold a Critter Capsule, the arc a throw would fly and where it lands, green when it would hit a critter you need. Taz measures your throws to learn Hypixel's arc. It only draws; aiming and throwing are yours.

## The route

A path through the whole run, one biome at a time, along the Safari's walkways (SkyHanni's map) and over the blocks for the last stretch. In each biome it goes to:

1. Critters of that biome in view, or where one was last seen.
2. Things you can do now: bee nests, Hideonwall perches, Snoozle and Troodon walls, the haunted house's beds, Rockmite mounds, the Cavernfish pond, the Polaris cave, the Shuddersquid pool, critter spots, Scrappy's food, and the Ritual, Gem Podiums, Birdfeeder or basement water once you carry what they need.
3. Search points round the biome for roaming critters, and floor drops when you still need quest items.

Once the route picks a target it sticks with it until it's done or gone. A floor drop within 16 blocks is a short detour. **Your biome** picks which biome comes first; on Auto it's your Hunting Hotspot, then the biome you're in, then the one with the most left.

## Quest items

- The Ritual takes a Soothing Incense for each of its 4 candles, and the Gem Podiums all 3 gems. Taz counts candles lit and gems placed, and sends you to floor drops until you have the rest.
- Scrappies eat one of three foods each run. Taz learns which from chat and sends you for it.
- Each quest item you carry shows on the HUD with what it's for, until its critters are caught.

## HUD and chat

- **Guide HUD:** critters caught, time left, capsules, hotspot, what's left in your biome and how to find each one. Caught critters and done biomes are left out.
- **Countdowns:** a Shyworm up (about 20 seconds), the Wumpa waking (15-25 seconds), Hideyho hiding, a Scrappy out of its shell, the Doomspiral.
- **Tips:** a welcome, each biome's tricks, quest items, low capsules, and the 10 and 2 minute warnings.
- **Alerts:** Sparkling critters, Macaws at the Birdfeeder, the Wumpa, the Doomspiral and Gemzies.
- **Sparkling callouts** (off by default): in a party, Taz says where a Sparkling critter is in party chat.
- **Run report:** when you leave, what you and your party caught, the rewards, your best run, and what you missed in each biome and why.
- **Critterdex sync:** open your Critterdex and every critter it shows counts as caught.

## How it works

Hypixel puts a name tag with each critter's name over its body. Catches come from chat: `CAPTURE!` lines are yours, `LOOT SHARE!` lines a party member's. Each Safari is a new world, so a run starts over by itself.
